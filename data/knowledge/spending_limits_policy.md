# Enterprise Procurement Spending Limits and Approval Threshold Policy
Document ID: POL-PROC-001
Category: Spending Limits & Approval
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Applies To: Wholesale, Distribution, and Retail Units

## 1. Objective and Authority
This policy establishes financial signing thresholds, authorization hierarchies, and governance controls for all procurement purchase orders across OMNIX-managed business entities. All purchases must adhere to the spending caps and authorization matrix detailed below.

## 2. Spending Thresholds & Authorization Matrix
1. **Tier 1 - Routine / Micro Orders (Up to ₹25,000.00)**:
   - Permitted Approvers: Store Supervisors, Warehouse Team Leads, or Procurement Officers.
   - Requirement: Single-tier digital approval. Reorder must align with standard minimum safety stock levels.

2. **Tier 2 - Operational Reorder (₹25,001.00 to ₹75,000.00)**:
   - Permitted Approvers: Operations Manager or Procurement Manager.
   - Requirement: Standard review against current 30-day sales velocity and approved supplier list. Auto-validation eligible if the supplier is designated as preferred and within standard lead time.

3. **Tier 3 - Substantial Orders (₹75,001.00 to ₹4,00,000.00)**:
   - Permitted Approvers: Senior Procurement Manager or General Manager.
   - Requirement: Minimum two competitive supplier quotes must be compared on price, delivery lead days, and reliability score before purchase order approval.

4. **Tier 4 - High-Value Critical Orders (Above ₹4,00,000.00)**:
   - Permitted Approvers: Vice President of Operations, Director, or Company Admin.
   - Requirement: Mandatory dual-authorization. Financial audit log recorded. Automated approval is strictly prohibited for orders exceeding ₹4,00,000.00 regardless of supplier preference or inventory stockout urgency.

## 3. Strict Prohibitions
- **Order Splitting**: Dividing a single procurement requirement into multiple lower-value purchase orders to circumvent Tier 3 or Tier 4 approval limits is strictly prohibited and constitutes a major compliance violation.
- **Unauthorized Commitments**: No verbal or informal purchase commitments may be given to vendors prior to system approval of the Purchase Order.
