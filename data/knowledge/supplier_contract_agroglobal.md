Document ID: CTR-SUP-AGRO-2026
Category: Supplier Contract
Version: 1.0

## 1. Parties and Contract Term
This Commercial Agreement is entered into between AgroBulk Logistics & Wholesale ("Buyer") and AgroGlobal Commodities Ltd ("Supplier"), registered at APMC Complex, Vashi, Navi Mumbai. Contract active from 1st January 2026 to 31st December 2027.

## 2. Product Supply Terms & Minimum Order Quantities
- **Basmati Rice Premium 25 kg (SKU-RICE-25KG)**:
  - Agreed Base Unit Price: ₹1,820.00 per 25 kg bag.
  - Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 50 bags (1,250 kg).
  - Promised Delivery Lead Time: 3 to 4 business days upon purchase order transmission.
- **Refined White Sugar 50 kg (SKU-SUGAR-50KG)**:
  - Agreed Base Unit Price: ₹2,180.00 per 50 kg bag.
  - Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 30 bags (1,500 kg).
  - Promised Delivery Lead Time: 4 business days upon order transmission.

## 3. Commercial Payment Terms & Penalties
- **Credit Terms**: Net 30 days from goods inspection and receipt note (GRN).
- **Early Settlement Discount**: 1.5% discount applicable if payment is processed within 10 business days of invoice verification.
- **Late Delivery Penalty**: If delivery is delayed beyond agreed lead days without written force majeure notification, a 0.5% invoice deduction per day of delay applies, capped at a maximum of 5% of order value.
- **Quality Rejection Protocol**: Rejections due to moisture content exceeding 12% or packaging tears must be notified within 48 hours of delivery; supplier must replace rejected goods within 3 calendar days at supplier's expense.
